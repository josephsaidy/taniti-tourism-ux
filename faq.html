Travel FAQs

Find the practical details visitors most often need before traveling.

What currency is used?

The U.S. dollar is Taniti’s currency. Some businesses accept euros and yen, and many accept major credit cards.

What power outlets are used?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same voltage as in the United States.

Is English spoken?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common among older residents in rural areas.

Are medical services available?

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics. Many hospital employees speak multiple languages.

What should visitors know about safety?

Violent crime is rare, but reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased as tourism grows.

Do attractions close on holidays?

Yes. Taniti has many national holidays, and some attractions and restaurants close on those days.

Are there alcohol restrictions?

Alcohol cannot be sold or served from midnight to 9 a.m. The legal drinking age is 18.

What food options are available?

Taniti has ten restaurants: five mostly local fish and rice, three American-style, and two Pan-Asian restaurants.
