Arriving on Taniti

Air

Arrive by plane

Most visitors fly into Taniti’s small airport, which currently accommodates small jets and propeller planes.

Sea

Arrive by cruise ship

One small cruise ship docks at Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Travel around the island

Public buses

Taniti City public buses run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.; private buses serve other areas of the island.

Rental cars & taxis

A rental agency is near the airport. Taxis are available in Taniti City.

Biking

Bike and helmet rentals are available. Helmets are required by law.

Walking

Taniti City is relatively flat and walkable. Merriton Landing is also easy to explore on foot.
